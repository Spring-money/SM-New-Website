About us

We build the software wealth firms run on.

Spring Money builds the software that IFAs and wealth managers run their firms on: integrated intelligence, the firm's own name in front, and a modern digital experience for its clients.

SKN
AMR

Why we exist.

A distribution or advisory firm in India runs on many systems at once: an exchange for orders, registrar feeds for holdings, a KYC service, a signing service, spreadsheets for reviews, and a messaging app for everything else. Its clients see a different brand on every one of them.

We think a firm should run its whole practice in one place, under its own name, and offer wealth management as personal as the family it serves.

That is what we build: Manthan, one platform with integrated intelligence and the firm's own brand in front of every client, and Spring Sense agents, rolling out to firms now, that prepare the work for people to approve.

What we believe, in one page.

Mission

Give every wealth advisor in India the intelligence to grow, and to know every client the way they know their best one.

Idea

One house. One company, one product, one design system. Every firm gets its own front door.

Position

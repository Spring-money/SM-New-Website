Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is Manthan one platform?

Yes. Your team and your clients work from the same records, so everyone sees the same picture.

Can we switch features on and off?

Yes. Manthan is customised per firm. You start with what your practice uses and add more as you grow.

Which exchanges and registrars does it work with?

BSE StAR MF, NSE and MF Utilities for mutual fund orders, and CAMS and KFintech feeds and CAS statements for holdings. More sources and products are planned with partners.

Will our clients see our brand?

Yes. Your clients see your name, logo, colours and font. Spring Money is a small technology credit.

Can clients transact themselves?

Where your firm switches it on, clients can buy, start a SIP or redeem. Payments go through payment links and mandates. The platform does not hold client money.

How do we bring existing clients across?

We bring your clients, holdings and history across from your current system, and show your team what changes on day one.

How is Manthan priced?

3 basis points (0.03%) a year of managed assets on the platform, billed monthly, plus GST. All capabilities and updates are included.

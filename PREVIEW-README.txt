Spring Money website, v1.1 preview
==================================

How to open
1. Unzip this folder.
2. Double-click index.html (open it in Chrome or Safari). Everything runs locally; no internet needed.
3. Use the top navigation to move between pages.

What changed since v1 (Omkar's feedback, 6 Oct 2026)
- The story now leads with value: integrated intelligence, your own brand, a modern digital experience and a configurable platform, for IFAs and wealth managers.
- No licence-wording section or toggle, no "three screens", no form-factor talk. Spring Sense says "live, rolling out to firms now" (AJ, 15 Oct). The MFD and RIA pages stay separate and are reframed.
- Firm-level demo figures are between Rs 300 Cr and 1,000 Cr. The "Your firm" and "Your family" icons in the asset orbit are new.
- The "For MFD firms" and "For RIA firms" pages are still here, reworded. Whether to merge them is open (see CHANGELOG-v1.1.md in the working folder).

What's in it
Home, Platform, Features (59, searchable), Spring Sense, For MFDs, For RIAs, Partners, Security,
About, Principles, Team, Clients, FAQ, Book a demo, plus Privacy, Terms and Grievances.

Does Spring Money give advice?

No. Advice is given by your firm. Spring Money is a technology provider. Your recommendations and plans are yours.

Will our clients see our brand?

Yes. Your clients see your name, logo, colours and font. Spring Money is a small technology credit.

Our firm holds both registrations. Can one platform handle both?

Yes, kept apart. Each client family sits under one registration only, and advisory and distribution records stay separate.

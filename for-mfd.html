A day in your distribution office.

The five jobs your team does every day, in one place.

1

Onboard

Self-onboarding with CKYC checks, and KYC status through MF Utilities.

2

See the whole family

CAMS and KFintech feeds, CAS statements and the account aggregator, so each family's holdings sit together.

3

Place orders

Purchases, SIPs and redemptions through BSE StAR MF, NSE or MFU, with eNACH and NACH mandates.

4

Reconcile

Orders and holdings matched to registrar feeds, with breaks cleared from the task centre.

5

Review

Portfolio statements and service reviews for each family, and scheme suggestions for your team to offer.

Orders and the back office, together.

The execution centre shows every order batch and what needs attention. The task centre keeps your operations team on one list.

  • Payment links. Clients pay through payment links. The platform does not hold client money.
  • Reconciliation. Breaks listed, assigned and cleared.
  • Messages. WhatsApp and email, and a document vault kept with each client.

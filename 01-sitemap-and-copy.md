## Spring Sense page: copy (proposal)
- Badge: "In use with firms today"
- **H1:** "Agents that prepare the work. People who approve it."
- **Lede:** "Spring Sense gives your firm personalised AI agents inside the planner portal. They draft, check and flag. Your adviser or distributor reviews, edits and approves. Nothing reaches a client until a person says so."
- **Three rules strip:** "A person approves before anything reaches a client." · "Every AI output is labelled AI-generated." · "Your named adviser or distributor stays responsible."
- **Five agents** (MFD wording / RIA wording):
  1. Meeting prep and notes: "A client brief before the meeting. A summary with actions after it." Same for both.
  2. Portfolio review drafts: MFD "Drafts the portfolio review and scheme suggestions for your distributor to edit and approve." RIA "Drafts the advisory review. Recommendations stay your adviser's own; the draft is a starting point to edit and approve."
  3. Client follow-ups: "Drafts reminders, SIP and birthday messages. You approve each one before it is sent."
  4. Ops and compliance checks: "Flags reconciliation breaks, KYC expiry and missing documents, so your team sees them first."
  5. Ask Spring Sense: "A general assistant that draws on your firm's historical and current client data to answer your team's questions."
- **Closing line:** "Spring Sense drafts and flags. It does not give advice, and it does not decide."
- Not on the page: accuracy claims, "AI adviser", where data is processed, which models, retention (questions for engineering are in the README).

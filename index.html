    Frequently asked questions

    Who is Manthan built for?

    SEBI-registered investment advisers (RIAs), mutual fund distributors (MFDs) and wealth practices, whether you advise, distribute or both.

    Will our clients see the Spring Money brand?

    Only as a small technology credit. Your firm's name, logo, colours and typography lead every screen.

    Does Spring Money sell investments or give advice?

    No. Spring Money is a technology provider. Your firm keeps full ownership of advice and distribution, under its own registration.

    What is Spring Sense AI?

    A set of AI capabilities that draft meeting briefs, prepare portfolio reviews and check compliance items. Nothing reaches a client without an advisor's approval.

    How is Manthan priced?

    3 basis points (0.03%) a year of managed assets on the platform, billed monthly, plus GST. All capabilities and updates are included.

    How do we see a live demo?

    Get in touch to book a private walkthrough. We'll prepare a demo in your firm's brand and set-up.

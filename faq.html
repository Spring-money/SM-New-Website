About Spring Money

What is Spring Money?

Spring Money is the technology partner for India's wealth advisors: firms that advise, distribute or both. Its platform, Manthan, gives them integrated intelligence, their own brand in front of clients and a platform customised to how they work.

Is Spring Money an investment adviser or a mutual fund distributor?

No. Spring Money is a technology provider. Your firm advises or distributes under its own registration. We build the tools your team and your clients use.

How does Spring Money make money?

Firms pay 3 basis points (0.03%) of the assets they manage on Manthan, calculated annually and billed monthly. We take no commission or sales-linked payment from fund houses or other product makers.

Where is Spring Money based?

Pune, at MIT TBI on the MIT WPU campus in Kothrud. The company is 2AN Technologies Private Limited.

For mutual fund distributors

What should a mutual fund distributor look for in software?

That depends on the firm. Spring Money is built for firms that want onboarding, orders on BSE StAR MF, NSE and MF Utilities, reconciliation and portfolio reviews, in their own brand, on one platform.

Can I place mutual fund orders through BSE StAR MF, NSE and MFU?

Yes. Purchases, SIPs, redemptions and mandates run through BSE StAR MF, NSE and MF Utilities, with an execution centre that shows every order batch.

Can we upgrade from our current system?

Yes. We bring your clients, holdings and history across, and show your team what changes on day one. Holdings come in from CAMS and KFintech feeds and CAS statements.

For investment advisers

What software do SEBI-registered investment advisers use?

Advisers need onboarding with an agreement, risk profiling, financial plans, recommendations, reviews and fee management. Spring Money brings these together, with the adviser's SEBI registration details where they belong.

Does Spring Money support fee-only advice?

Yes. Advisory firms get fees and invoices, with fee collection through UPI AutoPay or bank transfer, and partners come in a no-payout mode that fits fee-only advice.

Can one firm both advise and distribute?

Yes. Firms with both an RIA registration and an ARN run each client family in the right lane.

Clients and brand

Will my clients see my firm's brand?

Yes. Your clients see your name, logo, colours and font. Spring Money appears only as a small technology credit.

Can clients see all their investments in one place?

Yes. Everything a family owns, owes and is protected by sits in one picture. With the client's consent, accounts held elsewhere can come in through the account aggregator.

Does the platform hold client money?

No. Clients pay through payment links and mandates. The platform never holds client money or units.

Spring Sense

What is Spring Sense?

Personalised AI agents, rolling out to firms now. They prepare meeting briefs, draft portfolio reviews and client follow-ups, flag KYC expiry and reconciliation breaks, and answer questions from the firm's own records.

Does Spring Sense give investment advice?

No. It drafts and flags for your team. Your adviser or distributor approves anything before it reaches a client, and every output is labelled AI-generated.

Assets and products

Which assets does Spring Money cover?

The whole net worth. Mutual funds can be transacted today. Equities, ETFs, bonds, deposits, retirement savings (EPF, PPF, NPS), alternatives, physical assets, insurance and loans are tracked, through the account aggregator, registrar and exchange feeds, CAS statements or manual entry. Bonds, fixed deposits and loans against mutual funds are planned with partners.

Does Spring Money offer digital gold?

No. Digital gold is unregulated, so it is not on the platform.
